What We Do

Comprehensive Real Estate Services

Operating across Port Harcourt, Lagos, Abuja, and Owerri — Ultimate Home Finders manages every stage of property ownership and real estate investment.

Property Management

Full oversight for owners who can't personally oversee properties. We handle tenancy matters, resolve tenant issues, collect rent, and source qualified tenants.

Sales & Letting Agency

Acting as trusted agents for the sale of land and buildings, and for renting residential & commercial properties in Port Harcourt, Lagos, Abuja, and Owerri.

Buying Agency & Search

Representing buyers and tenants directly — sourcing verified properties for sale or rent tailored specifically to your budget and location preferences.

Investment Advisory

Using empirical market data to identify and rank high-ROI opportunities for capital deployment, followed by hands-on acquisition negotiation.

Small-Scale Estate Development

Taking on select, highly manageable development projects that deliver architectural quality, structural integrity, and long-term value.

Rentals Agency

Connecting serious tenants with verified landlords. Sourcing quality apartments, duplexes, office spaces, and commercial properties.
