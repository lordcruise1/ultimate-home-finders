Executive Biography

Engineering Integrity & Visionary Real Estate Leadership

Engr. Christopher Okoro founded Ultimate Home Finders in 1999 with a clear mission: to build a full-service real estate partnership founded on rigorous engineering discipline, absolute transparency, and deep local market insight in Port Harcourt and across Nigeria.

Founding Vision & Heritage

Over nearly three decades of active market leadership, Engr. Okoro has spearheaded the growth of Ultimate Home Finders from its Port Harcourt Head Office at Elekahia Housing Estate into a multi-city practice serving clients across major Nigerian real estate corridors including Port Harcourt, Lagos, Abuja, and Owerri.

Under his leadership, UHF has provided hands-on management for hundreds of residential and commercial properties, protected diaspora investors from title fraud, and structured high-return real estate investment acquisitions.

Engineering Approach to Property Management

As a qualified professional engineer, Engr. Okoro brings a technical, detail-oriented perspective to property evaluation, tenant management, structural assessments, and small-scale estate developments. This engineering background ensures that every property managed or developed by UHF adheres to high safety, aesthetic, and financial performance standards.

Expanding Footprint Across Nigeria

While maintaining a strong operational anchor at Block 16, Flat 1, 2nd Avenue, Elekahia Housing Estate, Port Harcourt, Engr. Okoro has expanded UHF's advisory and agency services to meet growing demand in key commercial centers:

  • Port Harcourt (Head Office): Full property management, land sales, tenant sourcing, and estate development.
  • Lagos: High-yield commercial acquisitions, residential buying agency, and investment portfolio advisory.
  • Abuja: Strategic land acquisition, federal capital territory property search, and diaspora investment representation.
  • Owerri: South-East agency services, residential letting, and land title verification.

"Real estate transactions should never feel like a gamble. Our duty over 26 years has been to give property owners, buyers, and investors complete certainty through empirical market data and unyielding integrity."

— Engr. Christopher Okoro, CEO
