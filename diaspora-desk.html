The Problem We Solve

Real Estate in Nigeria, Without the Risk of Distance

For Nigerians abroad, buying property at home carries three distinct risks that don't apply to local buyers: you can't physically inspect, you're often relying on a proxy you can only partially verify, and documentation problems surface only after money has moved. The UHF Diaspora Desk addresses each of these — not with promises, but with a structured, documented process that mirrors what we do for our highest-value local clients.

Common Questions

Diaspora Client FAQ

How do currency and payment logistics work for diaspora buyers?

Transactions are denominated in Naira (₦) and settled locally. Most diaspora clients transfer to a trusted family member or solicitor in Nigeria who handles local disbursements — or use a reputable international remittance service (Wise, Remitly, etc.) to a Nigerian domiciliary account. We do not handle client funds directly; we facilitate and document the transaction, and can refer you to our vetted network of solicitors who do handle escrow and documentation formalities.

Can I use a Power of Attorney to complete a purchase without traveling?

Yes. A notarised Power of Attorney (POA) is the standard mechanism for diaspora buyers completing Nigerian property transactions without physical presence. We strongly recommend engaging a qualified Nigerian property solicitor to draft or review your POA before granting it. Our vetted professional referral network includes solicitors experienced in diaspora POA arrangements, and we can connect you directly on request.

How am I kept updated during a transaction if I'm in a different timezone?

The Diaspora Desk operates with time-zone coverage in mind. Communication is managed primarily via WhatsApp (voice notes, photos, documents, and video — all asynchronous-friendly), with scheduled video call checkpoints at stages that require real-time confirmation. You will never be left guessing about where a transaction stands — status updates are proactive, not reactive.

What if I can't physically travel to Nigeria to inspect a property before purchase?

This is exactly what the scheduled video walkthrough service is built for. We physically visit the property ourselves before your walkthrough call, and will not present a property for a live tour that we haven't already assessed in person. For investment land specifically, we also provide a dry-land and flood-risk assessment (a common concern in Port Harcourt's growth corridors) as part of the walkthrough documentation.

What types of properties does UHF source for diaspora clients?

We serve diaspora clients across our full service range: residential purchase (family homes, duplexes), investment land banking in Port Harcourt's growth corridors, residential rentals for family members still in Nigeria, and property management for clients who already own property but need reliable on-the-ground oversight while abroad. The Diaspora Desk is a service layer, not a separate property category — any property on our books can be structured for a diaspora acquisition.

Which cities do you operate in for diaspora clients?

Our head office is in Port Harcourt (Elekahia Housing Estate, Rivers State), and we have active agency and advisory operations in Lagos, Abuja, and Owerri. Diaspora Desk services are available across all four cities. Port Harcourt and its growth corridors (Eneka, Rukpokwu, Igwuruta, Omagwa, Airport Road axis) are our deepest market — see our Market Intelligence Report for a detailed comparison of Port Harcourt versus Lagos for mid-capital land investors.
